Khanmigo or CoreMark Coach: which AI tutor suits a Cambridge Lower Secondary student, and where can you even get it?

Khanmigo is the best-known AI tutor from a non-profit with a large free library. Coach is built around one Cambridge stage. They answer different questions.

CoreMark makes CoreMark Coach, so we have a stake here. Khanmigo is made by Khan Academy, and CoreMark has no connection to it. Everything we say about Khanmigo comes from its own pages, checked on 20 October 2026, and we say where we could not find something.

Parents in India often ask about Khanmigo first, because Khan Academy is a name they trust. The comparison is genuinely lopsided in some places and in others it is simply two different jobs. We start with the question that decides most cases: can you buy it at all?

What is Khanmigo?

Khanmigo is Khan Academy's AI tutoring assistant. Its own site calls it "your always-available tutor on just about every topic", and says it can help with "math, science, coding, history, and humanities" for students "from elementary school through college" (khanmigo.ai). It comes from a non-profit with a large library of free lessons and practice, which is a real advantage when your child wants a lesson as well as a conversation.

Parents of under-18 learners must sign up and add their child to the parent account, and the parent page says parents can view their children's chat history and get moderation alerts. That visibility is a strength worth noting.

Can a family in India use Khanmigo for students?

We could not find a way to. Khanmigo's parent page states a price of $4 a month or $44 for an annual plan, and says users must "live in the United States, and have a billing address in the U.S." (khanmigo.ai/parents). We found no student price or sign-up route for India on Khanmigo's own pages, and the pricing page does not name a currency.

What does exist in India is Khanmigo for Teachers. Khan Academy announced that it became available in India in Hindi and English, in an update dated 14 November 2024 (Khan Academy blog). That is a tool for teachers, not for a child studying at home. Policies change, so check Khan Academy's current pages before deciding.

Check before you compare features If you cannot sign up, the rest of this comparison is academic. Ask Khanmigo's support whether your location and payment method are accepted.

Does Khan Academy line up with Cambridge Lower Secondary?

We found no official Khan Academy page saying it aligns to Cambridge Lower Secondary, and no Cambridge Lower Secondary Maths course on the site (checked 20 October 2026). Khan Academy's material is not wasted for a Cambridge student, since much of the underlying maths overlaps. The difference shows in vocabulary, command words and the way marks are awarded, which Cambridge papers expect and general lessons do not teach. Our post on whether Khan Academy covers Cambridge Lower Secondary goes into the detail.

What does Khanmigo do better than Coach?

It covers far more ground. A student who wants help with history, coding or a subject outside Maths, Science and Computing has no equivalent in Coach. Khan Academy also gives children lessons and practice to move between, so the AI is one part of a larger library. Parents get visibility features that are clearly described on Khanmigo's pages. And for a family that can access it, the listed US price is lower than Coach's, though currency, taxes and availability limit that comparison.

What does a Cambridge-specific tutor change in practice?

It changes how an answer is written. A Cambridge paper awards marks for method, so a child who gets the right number with no working can lose marks, and a child with a slip in the last line can still earn most of them. A tutor that only checks final answers does not prepare a student for that.

Coach's Learn mode shows the exam-booklet layout every time. This is the format from its teaching file, using angles as the example:

Working laid out the way examiners expect (example)

Step 1: identify the rule (co-interior angles sum to 180 degrees). Step 2: write the equation (x + 40 = 180). Step 3: solve (x = 140 degrees). Final answer: x = 140 degrees [1 mark].

Your child then has to copy that layout in the next practice question. General tutors can teach the same maths, and some will do this if you ask, but you have to ask. The point of a tutor built for one stage is that the habit is already there.

When is Khan Academy plus Khanmigo enough?

It is enough when your child needs general concept help across several subjects, you can access Khanmigo from where you live, and the Cambridge-specific wording of exam questions is something a school or tutor already covers. It is also enough for a younger student who is not yet facing Checkpoint. If any of those three do not hold, a Cambridge-specific tool makes more sense.

What should you ask Khanmigo's support team before paying?

If you can reach Khan Academy, three questions settle most doubts. Ask whether students in your country can sign up with a local billing address. Ask whether any Cambridge Lower Secondary content is planned or listed. And ask what a parent can see, including whether you can read the full conversation or only alerts. Their answers will be more current than anything written here, because pricing and availability change.

Do the same with us. The Coach page lists what is included, and you can write to info@coremark.study with questions before you buy.

How do you decide in five minutes?

  1. Try to start a Khanmigo account as a family. If you cannot, the choice is made.
  2. Ask whether your child needs several subjects, or only Maths, Science or Computing.
  3. Look at one exam-style question your child got wrong. Does a general explanation fix it, or did they lose marks on working and wording?
  4. Decide who will run the account and sit in for early sessions.
