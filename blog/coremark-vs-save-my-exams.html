CoreMark vs Save My Exams for Cambridge Lower Secondary

Both are Cambridge revision resources. But they serve different curriculum levels entirely. Here is the honest comparison for parents of Stage 7, 8 and 9 students.

Quick answer

CoreMark and Save My Exams are not really competitors. CoreMark is built for Cambridge Lower Secondary (Stages 7–9, syllabus codes 0862/0893/0860) and focuses on topic-by-topic PDF practice boosters. Save My Exams covers Cambridge IGCSE and A-Level, not Lower Secondary. A Stage 8 student should use CoreMark now and graduate to Save My Exams when they reach IGCSE in Stage 10.

The short answer

When parents search "CoreMark vs Save My Exams," they are usually trying to answer one question: which should I buy for my Stage 7, 8, or 9 child right now? The answer is CoreMark — not because Save My Exams is inferior, but because Save My Exams does not cover Cambridge Lower Secondary at all. Its catalogue starts at IGCSE.

This is a curriculum-level mismatch, not a quality comparison. Both tools are well-made for their respective audiences. The mistake is buying the wrong one for the wrong stage.

What is CoreMark?

CoreMark — scope

coremark.study is a library of downloadable PDF practice boosters for Cambridge Lower Secondary students in Stages 7, 8 and 9. Each booster covers one topic — one cheat sheet, 20 levelled questions (Foundation to Stretch), and full worked solutions. Subjects: Maths (0862), Science (0893), Computing (0860). Individual boosters from ₹249; subject-stage bundles from ₹999.

CoreMark is designed for a specific gap: Cambridge Lower Secondary students who need focused practice on one topic at a time, rather than full-paper simulation. It complements Cambridge official past papers and specimen papers rather than replacing them.

What is Save My Exams?

Save My Exams — scope

savemyexams.com is an online revision platform covering Cambridge IGCSE (Maths 0580, Biology 0610, Chemistry 0620, Physics 0625, Computer Science 0478), Cambridge International AS & A Level, and UK GCSE and A-Level. Content includes topic notes, worked examples, past-paper questions, and mark-scheme breakdowns. Subscription-based with a free tier.

Save My Exams is excellent for its target audience. Their topic notes are clearly structured, their past-paper question banks are extensive, and their mark-scheme guidance is reliable. For a student sitting IGCSE in Year 10–11, it is one of the strongest revision tools available.

For a student in Cambridge Lower Secondary Stage 7, 8, or 9, their content simply does not exist.

Honest assessment

Save My Exams is one of the best revision platforms available — for IGCSE and A-Level. This article is not arguing otherwise. The issue is that it simply has no Cambridge Lower Secondary content, which is a fact rather than a criticism.

CoreMark's strength is its specificity: every question, every cheat sheet, and every worked solution is written against the Cambridge Lower Secondary curriculum framework for that exact topic and stage. A Stage 8 student revising Standard Form gets questions that mirror the command words, mark allocation, and difficulty range they will actually see in a Cambridge Checkpoint assessment. IGCSE content from another platform will not achieve this, because IGCSE Standard Form is covered at a different depth and with different conventions.

⚠️
Common mistake Some parents use IGCSE maths resources to supplement Lower Secondary, reasoning that the content overlaps. In Number and basic Algebra it does, partially. But in Geometry, Statistics and all of Science and Computing the frameworks diverge significantly, making IGCSE practice unreliable for Stage 7–9 students.

The switching moment

CoreMark and Save My Exams are not alternatives. They are sequential tools for the same Cambridge path:

  • Stage 7–9 (ages 11–14): Use CoreMark for topic-specific practice and Cambridge official specimen papers for full-paper simulation.
  • Stage 10 (IGCSE, ages 14–16): Add Save My Exams. At this point their past-paper banks and topic notes become directly relevant. CoreMark no longer applies.
  • A Level (ages 16–18): Continue with Save My Exams. Their A-Level content is strong.

Parents who have been using CoreMark for a Stage 9 student can expect to add Save My Exams when that student transitions to IGCSE in the following academic year. The two tools cover adjacent, non-overlapping stages of the Cambridge journey.

Verdict

For a student currently in Cambridge Lower Secondary Stage 7, 8, or 9: use CoreMark. Save My Exams has no content for this stage.

For a student in Cambridge IGCSE or A-Level: use Save My Exams. CoreMark does not cover IGCSE.

These are not competing tools. Buy both, at the right point in the curriculum journey.
